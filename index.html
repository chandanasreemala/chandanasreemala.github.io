About

I am a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Pisa and KDD Lab, working on Trustworthy AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, hallucination mitigation, explainability, and uncertainty estimation.

My research focuses on building faithful and robust generative AI systems, with particular interest in how large language models behave when they are supported by external knowledge, confidence signals, and explanation mechanisms.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a data scientist in Italy, developing machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, financial analytics, and deployment solutions for industrial use cases.
