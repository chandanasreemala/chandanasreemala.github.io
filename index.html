About

Researcher and former data scientist working at the intersection of AI reliability and real-world deployment.

Profile

My research focuses on designing trustworthy generative AI systems, particularly methods for evaluating and mitigating hallucinations in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. I work on faithfulness, confidence-aware explanations, adaptive RAG, and the behavioural reliability of large language models.

Before beginning my PhD, I worked as a data scientist on machine learning, computer vision, NLP, financial analytics, API development, and business intelligence solutions.
